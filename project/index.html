What You Can Do

BOM Journey provides simple tools that help make your scripture study meaningful, consistent, and engaging.

📖 Daily Reading

Read the Book of Mormon one chapter at a time using the built-in Scripture Reader. Mark each completed chapter and develop a consistent study habit.

🛂 Scripture Passport

Create your personal scripture passport, set a study goal, and save your progress on your device as you continue your Book of Mormon journey.

⭐ Character Spotlight

Discover faithful prophets, missionaries, kings, and disciples throughout the Book of Mormon and learn from their examples.

Why BOM Journey?

Our goal is to encourage consistent scripture study through simple, meaningful tools that help you stay engaged with the Book of Mormon.

Grow Spiritually

Read the Book of Mormon consistently, strengthen your faith in Jesus Christ, and deepen your understanding of His gospel through daily scripture study.

Stay Motivated

Track your reading progress, celebrate completed chapters, and develop lasting scripture study habits that continue beyond this project.
