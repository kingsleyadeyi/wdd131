Site Name

BOM Journey

Why this name?
BOM Journey stands for Book of Mormon Journey. The name reflects an interactive learning experience that encourages users to study the Book of Mormon through scripture reading, quizzes, character exploration, and progress tracking. The word "Journey" emphasizes personal spiritual growth and continuous learning.

Optional Domain:
bomjourney.org

Site Purpose

BOM Journey is an interactive scripture study website designed to help users deepen their understanding of the Book of Mormon. The website will include scripture-based quizzes, Book of Mormon character profiles, reading progress tracking, and a feedback form. The goal is to make scripture study engaging, organized, and motivating.

Target Audience

  • Members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints
  • Missionaries
  • Youth and Young Single Adults
  • Seminary and Institute students
  • Anyone interested in learning about the Book of Mormon

Scenarios

  • How can I test my knowledge of Book of Mormon characters?
  • How do I keep track of the chapters I have already studied?
